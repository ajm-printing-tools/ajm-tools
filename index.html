Layout

 
 

Two cards at 8.5 × 5.5 cm need 11 cm in one direction, but a landscape 4×6 sheet is only 10.16 cm tall and 15.24 cm wide. So in Landscape the two cards are turned 90° to keep the exact size. Portrait keeps them upright. Cutting marks (grey corner ticks and a dashed centre line) sit outside the cards.

4. Preview and print

In the print dialog, choose 4×6 in paper, set margins to None and scale to 100%.
